# Supergun

by Aron Sommer · engine: Unity 5.6 · added 2018-01-11 · last update unknown
CrazyGames rating: 9.2 (2914 up / 241 down)

## Description

Supergun is an intense and fast paced arcade game from the creators of the awesome Eagle Ride. In this title, you control a spaceship and your aim is to guide your ship through space and avoid the red squares.

Your ship moves and fires automatically – all you have to do is control the left and right movements. This might sound easy but the gameplay is fast and you must have great reactions!

As you progress you ship will move through the maze of squares – avoid the red square but try and hit the other colored squares as they provide bonus scores. Furthermore, watch out for the yellow and grey circles as they will provide a huge boost to your ships firepower such as a quad gun or spaceships that help and target the red squares. See how far you can progress and what high score you can register!
