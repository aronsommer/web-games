# Astro Rush

by Aron Sommer · engine: Unity 5.6 · added 2021-04-19 · last update unknown
CrazyGames rating: 9.2 (4714 up / 397 down)

## Description

Astro Rush is an intense fast-paced arcade game to race through space and dodge obstacles as long as possible.

Take control of two meteorites while you rush through a trippy galaxy.

Rotate the meteorites through the obstacles. If you fail once, you will have one meteorite left and switch from rotating to dodging controls. You can play in normal mode or in three different wireframe modes.

## Controls

- With 2 meteorites: press AD or left and right arrow keys to rotate
- With 1 meteorite: press AD or left and right arrow keys to dodge
- You can invert the controls in the main menu
