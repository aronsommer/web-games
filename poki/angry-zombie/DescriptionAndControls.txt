Angry Zombie is a survival killer action arcade shooter game.

You are the Angry Zombie and you love to kill humans and bears.

Kill them all with the pistol, flamethrower, machine gun, rocket launcher and the incredible rocket launcher minigun.

Avoid meteorites and falling humans and bears, collect stars and try to survive as long as you can!

● Very simple controls
● Addictive gameplay
● 5 different weapons
● Retro-styled 3D graphics

Controls:

Use arrow keys to shoot and walk, press space bar to restart
